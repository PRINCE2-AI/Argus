# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Argus to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity, background, or ability. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make
participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never publish another person's credentials, logs, or
  personal information.
- Credit others for their work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing private information without consent are not
tolerated. Security issues must be reported privately as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not disclosed publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that violate this
policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports can
be sent privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and kept confidential.
